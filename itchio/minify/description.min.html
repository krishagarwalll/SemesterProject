About

How to Get to Heaven is a hand-drawn 2D puzzle-platformer about a ghost who can't move on until they solve one last mystery. You'll piece together an unsolved murder through narrative dialogue, point-and-click investigation, and light platforming.

Made by a five-person student team as a collaborative project for the UTS Game Studio 1 subject. Originally pitched as a visual novel paced across three acts delving into the protagonist's curiosity, doubt and acceptance, we condensed it down into a shorter murder mystery.

Recognised as one of the semester's top projects, the game was nominated to feature in the UTS Tech Fest 2026 Games Showcase. It was enthusiastically received, capturing the attention of judges and players for its eriee and uncanny art style.
